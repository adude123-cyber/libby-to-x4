01find the reader

if crosspoint.local doesn't answer, type the ip shown on the reader's screen.

browser won't connect?

this page is https, the reader is plain http on your local network. browsers are careful about that.

  1. use chrome or edge and click allow when asked about devices on your local network.
  2. or save this page and open the file from your computer. it works the same.
  3. or skip the automation: upload the .acsm in the reader's file manager and press fulfill on the protected content card.

02get the .acsm from libby

  1. open libbyapp.com/shelf/loans in a desktop browser.
  2. on the loan, pick read with… → other options → epub.
  3. a small .acsm file downloads. that's the ticket, not the book.

no epub option? that title is kindle- or libby-only and can't go to the x4.
